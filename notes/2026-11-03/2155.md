**Goal:** Make the OCR cascade and the layout engine trustworthy on the real corpus before phase IV: bounded OpenMP, coverage-based OCR routing with hybrid native+OCR pages, an OSD orientation pre-pass, barcode and anonymous-font flags, one rotation frame per page, and correct tier-3 geometry on `/Rotate` pages.

**Architecture:** All changes are in the existing OCR cascade (`src/core/ocr.rs`), the layout font loader (`src/core/layout/fonts.rs`, `words.rs`) and the OCR package loader. The cascade keeps its three tiers and its `minTextLength` option but decides per page by coverage, and merges OCR words with native words instead of replacing them. Every behaviour gets a public fixture generated by `examples/gen_fixtures.rs` (the private corpus is evidence, never a committed test). Section numbers below refer to `notes/2026-11-03/1037.md`.

**Tech Stack:** Rust 2024, lopdf 0.46, tesseract-rs 0.4 (`detect_os`, `set_image`, `get_iterator`), `image` 0.25 (fixture rotation), napi 3 / @napi-rs/cli 3.2 (`--js`), ava 8 + oxc-node, tsgo, oxfmt.
