## What changed

1. **Builds** format their generated typings and go through `scripts/build.mjs`, which forwards
   `--target` and friends to `napi build` (a plain `&& oxfmt` chain broke every CI job, found by
   the WASI step).
2. **OpenMP** is pinned to one thread per worker by `ocr/index.js` unless the caller set it.
3. **Routing by coverage**: a page is OCR'd when its native text is under `minTextLength`, or covers
   under 2% of the page box while images cover over half or the stream paints 200+ path operators.
   **Hybrid pages** keep native words; OCR adds what they do not cover (IoU > 0.5 dedupe). `source`
   is the deepest tier that ran.
4. **Orientation**: upright pass stands at mean confidence ≥ 80; otherwise Tesseract's OSD decides;
   the 90/270/180 retry runs only when nothing read at ≥ 55.
5. **Fonts**: barcode-font words are `symbolic`; `bold`/`italic` are undefined for anonymous fonts.
6. **One frame per page**: OCR boxes are mapped back to the raster as painted, placed through the
   `Do` placement, and the whole page (OCR words, native words, placements) is turned once by the
   page's `contentRotation`: the turn carrying the most confident OCR text across images (tiles
   vote together; images with fewer than three words do not vote).
7. **Render tier** maps words into the page's own frame; its turn includes `/Rotate`. For every
   tier: displayed coordinates = reported frame turned clockwise by `rotate − contentRotation`.
8. Docs, decisions 12.1 and 12.2 recorded in `notes/2026-11-03/1037.md`, WASI typings.
