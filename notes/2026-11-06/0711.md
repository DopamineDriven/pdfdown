**Goal:** `invoice()` and a new `invoiceFromLayout(pages)` return the header fields of an invoice from `PageLayout` pages, for native PDFs, OCR'd scans and workbooks alike, each field with text, span and confidence, and the eval harness can score it.

**Architecture:** A new engine under `src/core/invoice/` reads pages as x-segmented lines, matches a language-tagged lexicon to labels, resolves values by anchor geometry through typed validators (dates, amounts, identifiers), finds parties by font and position, reconciles totals, and fills the existing `InvoiceResult`. `invoiceFromLayout` is the public seam; `invoice()` builds pages per document kind (native layout, the OCR cascade on the first and last page in the OCR build, `project()` for a workbook) and calls it after the embedded-XML track.

**Tech Stack:** Rust, napi-rs 3, lopdf (fixtures), ava 8 + tsgo type tests, `examples/gen_fixtures.rs`. No new crates.
